Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy covers the Pushtick website (this site) and Pushtick Uploader, the tool Pushtick uses to publish its own videos.

Who we are

Pushtick is run by Bilawal Ali in Pakistan. Contact: hello@pushtick.com.

This website

This site is a few static pages. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no analytics, and it does not collect personal information. Our host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Pushtick Uploader

Pushtick Uploader is used only by Pushtick, to upload Pushtick's own videos to Pushtick's own accounts. It is not available to other people. To do this it signs in to those accounts with their official APIs:

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): uploads videos, titles, descriptions and captions to the Pushtick channel.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta Graph API): publishes Reels to the Pushtick Facebook Page and the @pushtickhq Instagram account.
  • TikTok (TikTok Content Posting API): sends videos to the Pushtick TikTok account.

The only data the tool handles is: the access tokens for these accounts, the videos and their text, and the IDs/links of published posts. Tokens are stored encrypted as GitHub Actions secrets and are used only to publish. We do not collect, sell or share data about viewers or any other users, and we do not read comments, messages or analytics through these APIs.

By using YouTube features in this tool you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions, in Facebook's Business integrations settings, or in TikTok's app permissions.

Deleting data

To remove the tool's access, revoke it in the account settings above; we then delete the stored tokens. Questions: hello@pushtick.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
